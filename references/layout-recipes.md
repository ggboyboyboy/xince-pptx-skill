# 12 Reusable Slide Layouts

All pages use 16:9 ratio and at least 0.38in side margins. Every slide communicates one clear claim with a supporting visual.

| ID | Slide type | Recommended composition |
|---|---|---|
| A | Cover | Left bold headline + right equipment hero image, subtle circuit base |
| B | Market landscape | Left source-backed trend chart + right three short evidence points |
| C | Pain points | Three balanced photo cards, each one headline and one consequence |
| D | Solution overview | Software + hardware + mechanical engineering layers, with real connections |
| E | Engineering evidence | Design → implementation → measured validation |
| F | Product capabilities | 50–60% real product photography + up to four measurable capabilities |
| G | Competitive differentiation | Editable table: 4–6 factors × 2–4 alternatives |
| H | Business model | Customer need → delivery value → revenue → support |
| I | Team | Three or four consistently cropped headshots and role-fit summaries |
| J | Financial KPI | Three large verified metrics + one editable chart with units |
| K | Roadmap | Three–five milestones with dates, measurable acceptance criteria |
| L | Closing | Minimal navy chip/circuit backdrop + short memorable value proposition |

## Shared structure
For slides B–K, allocate about 7% height to chapter navigation, 9–15% to title and section label, 65–72% to evidence, and 10–12% to a single bottom conclusion.

## Details by layout
- **Cover:** no multi-chapter nav; headline max two lines; device image ~40–50% width; short presenter/date line only where supplied.
- **Market:** consistent units and dates; at most two charts; clearly distinguish historic values from forecasts.
- **Pain points:** 3 equal-size cards; photo about 60% of card; one headline plus one sentence each.
- **Solution:** arrows represent actual data/control/mechanical flows, not decoration; annotate engineering interfaces.
- **Evidence:** preserve legibility of PCB, diagrams and lab screens; label stage, date, model, and test conditions.
- **Product:** large product visual plus 2–4 measured points; use explicit placeholders when photos are unavailable.
- **Comparison:** use real editable comparison table; colors must supplement, not replace, written pass/fail labels.
- **Model:** favor a simple left-to-right four-step causal chain over decorative hexagon walls.
- **Team:** consistent portrait cropping, consistent name/role hierarchy, no invented credentials.
- **KPI:** separate actual versus projected values; include currency, period and assumptions.
- **Roadmap:** each milestone needs date, owner and a measurable outcome; maps only for real geographic plans.
- **Closing:** no nav, maximum two lines of large text, contact info only when provided.

Use `refined` by default: at most three substantial modules per slide. If information does not fit, add a slide rather than shrinking text.
